This compendium synthesizes content from the following reference works to address the 74-topic Bulgarian state board examination syllabus in Orthopedics and Traumatology:
1.       AO Principles of Fracture Management, 3rd ed. (Buckley, Moran, Apivatthakakul)
2.       Apley & Solomon’s System of Orthopaedics and Trauma
3.       Dutton’s Orthopaedic Examination, Evaluation and Intervention
4.       Gray’s Anatomy
5.       Miller’s Review of Orthopaedics

6. Netter’s Concise Orthopaedic Anatomy
7. Netter’s Orthopaedic Clinical Examination
8. Operative Hip Arthroscopy (Byrd)
9. “Ortho Copy” (low-resolution scanned reference)
10. Orthopaedic Surgical Approaches
11. Rockwood and Green’s Fractures in Adults
12. Rothman-Simeone The Spine, 6th ed.

## Tachdjian’s Pediatric Orthopaedics

Each topic is written as flowing prose (Fliesstext) organized by subtopic — anatomy, pathophysiology, clinical presentation, imaging, treatment, complications — with content synthesized across all relevant sources.
